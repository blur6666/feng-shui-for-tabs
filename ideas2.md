# Backlog Ideas - Part 2
 
---
 
## 6. Smart Pruning & "Tab Bankruptcy" (Spring Cleaning)
* **1-Click Tab Bankruptcy:** For users overwhelmed with 100+ tabs, 1-click exports an AI-generated Markdown/HTML digest with 1-sentence summaries of all tabs, saves them to a timestamped bookmark folder, and cleanly closes them.
* **Auto-Collapse Inactive Groups:** Automatically collapses tab groups whose tabs haven't been clicked in the last 2 hours to keep the tab strip slim and focused.
* **Content Redundancy Detector:** Flags tabs with duplicate/overlapping information (e.g. 3 tabs of the same API documentation or product variations). ⭐

---

## 7. Multi-Monitor & Spatial Routing
* **Window-Per-Context Allocation:** Intelligently distribute tab groups across physical monitors or windows:
  - *Main Monitor:* `Active Coding / Work / Primary Task`
  - *Secondary Monitor:* `Documentation / Slack / Music / References`
* **"Zen / Focus Mode" Isolation:** Temporarily extracts only the 2–3 tabs needed for the current focus task into a clean, distraction-free window, leaving the rest grouped in the background.

---

## 8. Natural Language Command Bar ("Ask Your Tabs") ⭐
Allow users to type actions or semantic queries directly into the input box:
* *"Where was that article with the chart on interest rates?"*
* *"Group everything related to my trip to Tokyo and move it to a new window."*
* *"Close all shopping tabs except the cheapest flight."*

---

## 9. Session Workspace Packaging & Export
* **Named Workspaces:** Save an entire grouping setup as a workspace (e.g., `Home Renovation`, `Q3 Tax Prep`) and restore all associated tabs in their respective groups in 1-click.
* **Shareable Research Digest:** Export a tab group as a Notion/Markdown summary table with bullet points extracted from each page to share with teammates.

---

## 10. Proactive "Feng Shui" (Automated Triggers)
* **Clutter Threshold Alert:** Triggers or offers a clean-up prompt when unorganized tabs exceed a custom threshold (e.g., `> 15 unorganized tabs`).
* **End-of-Day Wind Down:** An optional 5/6 PM prompt to organize, archive, or bookmark the day's tabs for a fresh, clean start tomorrow.
